# Duel spectating in Munolith

Doorkeeper Titus shows all four arenas in a fitted desk. Each card identifies the fighters or an empty room and keeps the native Watch availability. The list can scroll at smaller sizes. Close, Escape and title dragging retain native behavior.

While spectating, the bottom score strip shows both complete fighter names, numeric scores and the native animated health/shield bars. Its exit button retains the game's channel-quit action and tooltip. A wider viewer roster stays above its native bottom anchor and includes every reported viewer.

The presentation does not change duel rules, score calculation, viewer limits or join/leave requests. Legacy and Modern keep their original presentation.

## Match scorecards
The native duel and guild-match scoreboards use compact green/gold cards. Names appear on the left and live scores on the right; blue/red identity colours and guild emblems remain visible. The cards stay behind inventory and other windows and accept no pointer input.

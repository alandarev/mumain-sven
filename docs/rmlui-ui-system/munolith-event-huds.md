# Running events in Munolith

Blood Castle and Chaos Castle show their event name, native monster/player counter and remaining time. Imperial Guardian shows its round and zone, standby or remaining-monster caption and countdown. Their color changes follow the game's urgency states.

Doppelganger combines monsters passed, countdown and the original progress path. Monster progress, the Ice Walker and every native party marker remain visible and update as before. These passive cards stay above the compact HUD at the right edge and leave event controls and gameplay unchanged.

Legacy and Modern keep their original presentation.

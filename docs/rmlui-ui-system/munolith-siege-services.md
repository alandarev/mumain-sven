# Castle services in Munolith

The hunting-zone gatekeeper presents the native access rules and entrance fee in a larger scrolling desk. Guild masters retain the public-access setting, fee adjustment and Confirm; members and guests see their native entry conditions. Closed access and insufficient Zen retain the game's existing behavior. Enter and Confirm act immediately as before.

The castle gate switch places the native gate artwork beside its instructions and warning. Its action label follows the open/closed gate state. Operating the switch retains the native permission/request path.

The catapult desk shows every attack or defense target as a named card. A selected target is highlighted and unlocks Shoot. No target selected keeps Shoot locked. Target identities and firing behavior are unchanged.

All three fit the safe viewport, retain readable text, scroll overflowing instructions and can be dragged by their title. Close and Escape retain the game's native behavior.

The castle siege HUD uses a compact green/gold map and command card above the main HUD. Native team and order selection, map transparency and battle-skill arrows remain available for the corresponding siege role. The remaining siege time sits above the map; command markers retain their team colours and native icons.
